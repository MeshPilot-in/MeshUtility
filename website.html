Supercharge Your AI Workflow

Built for developers, researchers, and creators who value speed, configuration, and privacy.

🎙️

Offline Voice Dictation

Hold your hotkey, speak, and release. Local Whisper models transcribe your words instantly and inject them into any active text field across any Windows application.

✨

Prompt Enhancer

Select text anywhere, trigger the global shortcut, and watch our floating overlay stream optimized, rewritten prompts ready to copy or replace inline.

🔒

100% Local Privacy

Optionally run fully offline. All configurations, prompt histories, and custom pronunciation dictionaries are kept in a local SQLite database on your device.

⚙️

Multi-Provider Support

Connect seamlessly to Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral, or any custom local/remote OpenAI-compatible API endpoint.

📥

System Tray Resident

Minimal footprint. Lives quietly in your system tray, consuming near-zero resources, ready to trigger instantly with global Windows hotkeys.

📚

Pronunciation Dictionary

Define custom mappings for complex terms, brand names, or specialized acronyms to train the transcription engine to match your voice precisely.

Tailored To Your Workspace

MeshUtility features a custom UI engine supporting beautiful dark and light themes out of the box.
